This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play Deep Bounce (Android package com.deepbounce.game), why it is processed, who processes it, and the choices you have. The game itself does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The game does, however, include third-party services from Google — advertising (Google AdMob with the User Messaging Platform) and purchases (Google Play Billing) — that process certain data as described below.

1. Information stored by the game on your device

Your game progress is saved in a file inside the app's private storage on your device: settings (language, sound, vibration), crystals, records, level stars, the run in progress, the items you own and a technical ledger of completed purchases and rewards (used so that a purchase or reward is applied exactly once). The game also keeps a small diagnostic log on the device with technical events (for example, "billing connected" or "ad failed to load"). This information stays on your device. It is not sent to the developer, and the developer has no server that receives it. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version of Deep Bounce shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for a second chance in a run or to double crystals) and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads between runs or levels. To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process the following data:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers: Android advertising ID, App set IDAdvertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate location derived from your IP addressAdvertising (regional targeting and compliance), fraud prevention
Ad interactions (ad requests, impressions, clicks, rewards)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (device model, OS version, app version, performance, crash information related to ads)Analytics, fraud prevention, maintaining the service

Whether ads are personalized depends on your consent and your device settings. This data is processed by Google as an independent controller or processor under its own terms. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

When you start the game, it uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to determine whether consent is required in your region (for example, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, or applicable US state privacy laws). If required, a consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and the game requests ads only when the consent status permits it. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Options (shown where applicable).

4. In-app purchases — Google Play Billing

Optional purchases (the Ad-Free pack, cosmetic ball and cannon finishes, and voluntary support contributions) are processed entirely by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment card or bank details. The game receives from Google Play only the information needed to deliver the item: the product identifier, the purchase state, a purchase token and an order identifier. This is stored on your device as described in section 1. Google's processing of payment data is governed by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The game does not use browser cookies. The Google Mobile Ads SDK uses device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and local storage on the device, which serve a similar purpose to cookies for advertising and measurement.

6. Why the information is processed (legal bases)

  • To provide the game and keep your progress — performance of the service you requested.
  • To show ads that fund the free game — your consent where the law requires it (for personalized ads), otherwise legitimate interest in offering the game free of charge.
  • To deliver purchases and prevent fraud — performance of a contract and legitimate interest.

7. Sharing

The developer does not sell your personal information and does not share it with anyone. Data processed by the Google SDKs listed above is shared with Google for the purposes described. No other third-party services are integrated into the game.

8. Retention

Game data on your device is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's retention policies (policies.google.com/technologies/retention). The developer does not hold copies of either.

9. Security

Data exchanged between the Google SDKs and Google's servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Game data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access on a non-modified device.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw ad consent in Settings → Privacy Options inside the game (where applicable).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads).
  • Remove all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.
  • Stop all advertising by purchasing the optional Ad-Free pack; the advertising SDK is then not started.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Because the developer does not hold personal data about you, requests concerning advertising data are best directed to Google (Google Data & Privacy); you can also contact the developer using the details above.

11. Children's privacy

Deep Bounce is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the developer and it will be addressed.

12. International transfers

Google may process data in countries other than your own, using safeguards described in Google's Privacy Policy.

This policy may be updated when the game or the integrated services change. The current version is always published at this address with its effective date. Significant changes will be reflected in the game's store listing.
